# Annual Leave Policy

Northgate Haulage Ltd. Version 2.0 - effective 1 January 2026. Owner: People Manager.

Reference: CANARY-NG-LEAVE-4d2f

## Entitlement

Full-time employees receive **28 days** of paid annual leave per year, plus bank holidays.

## Booking

Book leave in the Rota app at least **one week** in advance. Your depot supervisor approves it.

## Carry-over

You may carry over up to **3 days** into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by **28 February**.
