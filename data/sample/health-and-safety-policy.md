# Health and Safety Policy

Aldermoor Freight Ltd. Version 6.0 - effective 1 April 2026. Owner: Operations Director.

## 1. Responsibilities

The Operations Director is accountable for health and safety across the company. Each **Site Manager** is responsible for health and safety at their site, including risk assessments, fire safety, and first aid cover.

Every employee must work safely, follow site rules, and report hazards.

## 2. Yard and depot rules

- High-visibility clothing and safety boots must be worn at all times in yards, loading bays, and the workshop.
- The speed limit for all vehicles in our yards is **5 mph**.
- Pedestrians must use marked walkways. Never walk behind a reversing vehicle.
- Forklift trucks may only be driven by staff holding a current Aldermoor forklift licence.

## 3. Drivers

- Complete a **walkaround check** of your vehicle and trailer before every shift and record it in the driver app. Do not drive a vehicle with a safety defect; report it to the workshop.
- Never use a handheld phone while driving, including when stopped in traffic.
- Take the breaks required by the driving-hours rules. If you are too tired to drive safely, stop and tell your transport planner.

## 4. Reporting accidents and near misses

Report every accident, injury, and near miss within **24 hours** using the **SafeSite** app or the paper form in each site office. Near misses matter: they show us where the next accident could happen.

Serious injuries must also be reported to your Site Manager immediately by phone.

## 5. First aid

Every site has trained first aiders on each shift; their names are posted next to each first aid box. Call 999 in an emergency before calling anyone else.

## 6. Fire safety

Fire alarms are tested every Wednesday at 10:00. When the alarm sounds at any other time, leave by the nearest exit and go to your site's assembly point. Do not return until the fire marshal says it is safe.

## 7. Lone working

Staff working alone out of hours, including drivers on night trunking routes, must check in with the transport office at the times set out in their route plan.

## 8. Training

All new employees complete site safety induction on their first day. Refresher training is every two years.
