# Expense Policy

Aldermoor Freight Ltd. Version 2.3 - effective 1 February 2026. Owner: Finance Director.

## 1. Principles

Spend company money as if it were your own. You will be reimbursed for reasonable, necessary costs incurred on company business, in line with the limits below.

## 2. How to claim

- Submit every claim in **SpendTrack**, our expenses system.
- Submit claims within **30 days** of the date the cost was incurred. Claims older than 30 days are not paid unless the Finance Director approves the delay.
- Attach a **receipt for any item over £10**. Card statements are not accepted as receipts.
- Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary.

## 3. Approval

| Claim total | Approver |
|-------------|----------|
| Up to £500 | Your line manager |
| Over £500 | The Finance Director |

You may not approve your own claim or a claim from someone who approves yours.

## 4. Travel

### Rail and air

Book standard-class rail. First-class travel is not reimbursed. Air travel within the UK needs Finance Director approval before booking.

### Mileage

If you use your own car for business journeys you can claim:

- **45p per mile** for the first 10,000 business miles in a tax year
- **25p per mile** after that

Commuting between home and your normal place of work is not business mileage.

### Hotels

When an overnight stay is necessary, the maximum nightly rate (room only) is:

- **£150 per night** outside London
- **£190 per night** in London

## 5. Meals

- **Day trips** (no overnight stay, away for more than 5 hours): up to **£12** for lunch.
- **Overnight travel**: up to **£35 per day** for all meals.

Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## 6. Customer entertainment

Entertaining customers needs prior approval from your line manager. List the attendees and the business purpose in your claim.

## 7. Equipment

Equipment for home working is claimed under the allowance in the Remote Work Policy, using SpendTrack and following the rules in section 2 of this policy.

## 8. Misuse

Fraudulent or inflated claims are gross misconduct. Contact expenses@aldermoor.example.com with any questions.
