# Services Agreement - Fleet Cleaning

Contract reference: AF-PRC-2025-014

## 1. Parties

This agreement is between **Aldermoor Freight Ltd** ("Aldermoor") and **CleanFleet Services Ltd** ("the Supplier"), a vehicle cleaning company registered in England.

## 2. Term

The agreement starts on **1 April 2025** and ends on **31 March 2027**. It may be extended by written agreement of both parties.

## 3. Services

The Supplier will provide exterior washing and interior cab valeting for Aldermoor's HGVs at the **Daventry** and **Leeds** depots.

Service levels:

- Every vehicle in the agreed schedule is washed at least once every **14 days**.
- Ad-hoc cleaning requests are carried out within **48 hours** of the request.
- Cleaning takes place between 18:00 and 06:00 so vehicles are not taken off the road during operating hours.

## 4. Fees

Aldermoor will pay a fixed fee of **£4,200 per month**, excluding VAT, invoiced monthly in arrears. Ad-hoc requests beyond the schedule are charged at £45 per vehicle.

Invoices are payable within 30 days of receipt.

## 5. Service credits

If more than **3 scheduled vehicles** are not cleaned on time in a calendar month, the Supplier will credit **5%** of that month's fixed fee against the next invoice.

## 6. Termination

Either party may end this agreement for convenience by giving **60 days' written notice**. Either party may end it immediately if the other commits a material breach that is not fixed within 14 days of written notice.

## 7. Insurance and safety

The Supplier must hold public liability insurance of at least £5,000,000 and follow each depot's site safety rules, including the vehicle movement rules in the yard.
