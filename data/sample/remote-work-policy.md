# Remote Work Policy

Aldermoor Freight Ltd. Version 1.4 - effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Head of People.

## 1. Who this applies to

Remote work is available to **office-based roles** that have **completed probation**. Depot, warehouse, workshop, and driver roles are not eligible because the work must be done on site.

## 2. How much remote work

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to **3 days per week**, agreed with their line manager. Teams may set fixed office days for collaboration; your manager will tell you if this applies.

## 3. Core hours

When working remotely you must be available during core hours, **10:00 to 15:00**, Monday to Friday, and keep your calendar up to date.

## 4. Where you can work from

You must work from a private, safe location in the UK. Working from outside the UK is limited to **10 working days per leave year** and needs written approval from the People team before you travel, because of tax and data-protection rules.

Do not work on confidential customer information in public places such as cafes or trains where your screen can be seen.

## 5. Equipment and the home office allowance

Aldermoor provides a laptop, headset, and mobile phone where your role needs one.

Eligible employees can claim a **one-off home office allowance of £300** towards a desk, chair, monitor, or similar equipment. Claim it through SpendTrack, following the Expense Policy rules on deadlines and receipts. The allowance cannot be claimed again if you change roles.

## 6. Security

Always connect through the company **VPN** when working remotely, and follow the IT Security Policy. Do not let family members or others use your company devices.

## 7. Changes

Your manager may end or change a remote working arrangement with **two weeks' notice** if business needs change or if performance is affected.

## 8. Questions

Contact the People team at hr@aldermoor.example.com.
