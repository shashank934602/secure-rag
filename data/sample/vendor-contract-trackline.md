# Services Agreement - Vehicle Telematics

Contract reference: AF-PRC-2024-021

## 1. Parties

This agreement is between **Aldermoor Freight Ltd** ("Aldermoor") and **TrackLine Systems Ltd** ("the Supplier"), a provider of vehicle tracking and telematics services registered in England.

## 2. Term

The agreement starts on **1 July 2024** and ends on **30 June 2027**.

## 3. Services

The Supplier provides a tracking unit in each of Aldermoor's HGVs and access to its online fleet dashboard, including:

- live vehicle location
- driver behaviour reports (harsh braking, speeding, idling)
- fuel consumption reports per vehicle
- tachograph data download

The dashboard must be available at least **99.5%** of the time in each calendar month, excluding planned maintenance announced 5 days in advance.

## 4. Fees

Aldermoor pays **£18 per vehicle per month**, excluding VAT, for every vehicle with an active tracking unit. Installation of each unit costs a one-off £95.

Invoices are issued monthly in advance and are payable within **30 days** of the invoice date.

## 5. Data protection

Vehicle location data identifies drivers, so it is personal data. The Supplier acts as a data processor for Aldermoor and must:

- process the data only on Aldermoor's written instructions
- keep location history for **6 months** and then delete it, unless Aldermoor asks for specific records to be kept for an investigation
- store all data in the UK
- report any data breach to Aldermoor within **24 hours** of discovering it

## 6. Termination

Either party may end this agreement for convenience by giving **120 days' written notice**. On termination the Supplier removes the tracking units free of charge and returns all Aldermoor data within 30 days.
