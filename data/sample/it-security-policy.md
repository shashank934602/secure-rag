# IT Security Policy

Aldermoor Freight Ltd. Version 5.1 - effective 1 March 2026. Owner: Head of IT and Security.

## 1. Purpose

This policy protects our systems, our customers' shipment data, and our employees' personal data. It applies to everyone who uses an Aldermoor device or account, including contractors.

## 2. Passwords and sign-in

- Passwords must be at least **14 characters** long. Passphrases of four or more random words are recommended.
- Never reuse your Aldermoor password on any other site.
- **Multi-factor authentication (MFA)** is mandatory for email, the VPN, PeopleHub, and SpendTrack.
- Never share your password or MFA codes with anyone, including the IT Service Desk. IT will never ask for them.

## 3. Devices

- Company laptops have full-disk encryption enabled. Do not disable it.
- Lock your screen whenever you leave your device. Screens lock automatically after **5 minutes** of inactivity.
- Only IT may install software. Request new software through the IT Service Desk.
- Personal USB storage devices must not be connected to company devices.
- Do not leave laptops visible in vehicles, including in HGV cabs.

## 4. Email and phishing

Be suspicious of unexpected emails asking you to click links, open attachments, change bank details, or pay invoices. Changes to a supplier's bank details must always be confirmed by phone using a number already on file, never one given in the email.

Use the **Report Phishing** button in Outlook to report suspicious emails.

## 5. Reporting incidents

Report any of the following **within 1 hour** of noticing it:

- a lost or stolen laptop, phone, or tablet
- a suspected phishing email you clicked or replied to
- a password you think someone else knows
- any data sent to the wrong person

Report to the IT Service Desk on **555-0150** or by email to **security@aldermoor.example.com**. Out of hours, the Service Desk phone line is answered by the on-call engineer.

Reporting quickly matters more than anything else. Nobody is disciplined for honestly reporting a mistake.

## 6. Training

All employees must complete security awareness training within **30 days of joining** and once a year after that. Completion is tracked in PeopleHub.

## 7. Breaches of this policy

Deliberate breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action. Questions go to the IT Service Desk.
