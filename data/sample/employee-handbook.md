# Aldermoor Freight Ltd - Employee Handbook

Version 4.2 - effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Head of People.

## 1. About Aldermoor Freight

Aldermoor Freight Ltd is a road haulage and warehousing company founded in 1998. We move containerised and palletised freight between UK ports and distribution centres for retail and manufacturing customers.

We employ around 420 people across three sites:

- **Felixstowe** - head office and port operations, 12 Quayside Road, Felixstowe IP11 0AF
- **Daventry** - national distribution depot and main vehicle workshop
- **Leeds** - northern distribution depot

Our fleet is 180 heavy goods vehicles (HGVs) and 260 trailers.

## 2. Working hours

The standard working week for office staff is 37.5 hours, Monday to Friday. Office hours are 08:30 to 17:00 with a one-hour unpaid lunch break.

Depot, warehouse, and driver roles work rotating shift patterns. Your shift pattern is set out in your contract and published on the depot rota at least two weeks in advance. Drivers must follow the legal driving-hours rules and the Driver Operations Manual at all times.

## 3. Probation

All new employees complete a six-month probation period. Your line manager will hold reviews at three months and six months. Probation can be extended once, by up to three months.

## 4. Pay

Salaries are paid monthly by bank transfer on the last working day of each month. Payslips are published in the PeopleHub portal. Overtime for shift roles is paid one month in arrears.

## 5. Policies you must read

Every employee must read and follow these policies, all available in PeopleHub:

- Annual Leave Policy
- Sickness Absence Policy
- Expense Policy
- IT Security Policy
- Health and Safety Policy
- Remote Work Policy (office roles only)

## 6. Code of conduct

We expect everyone to treat colleagues, customers, and suppliers with respect. Harassment, bullying, and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated and will be dealt with under the disciplinary procedure.

You must declare any gift or hospitality from a supplier worth more than £50 to the Head of Procurement within five working days. Gifts of cash are never acceptable.

Smoking and vaping are allowed only in the marked shelters at each site. Zero tolerance applies to alcohol and drugs for anyone driving or operating equipment.

## 7. Raising concerns

If something is wrong, tell your line manager first. If that is not appropriate, contact the People team at hr@aldermoor.example.com. Concerns about wrongdoing can also be raised confidentially with the Finance Director under the whistleblowing procedure.
