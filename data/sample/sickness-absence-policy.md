# Sickness Absence Policy

Aldermoor Freight Ltd. Version 2.1 - effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Head of People.

## 1. Scope

This policy explains what to do if you are too ill to work, and what you are paid while you are off sick. It applies to all employees.

## 2. Telling us you are sick

- **Office staff:** phone your line manager by **09:00** on the first day of absence.
- **Shift staff and drivers:** phone your shift supervisor at least **1 hour before your shift starts**, so cover can be arranged.
- Text messages and emails are not enough on the first day; you must speak to someone.
- Keep your manager updated at least every three working days while you are off.

## 3. Evidence

For absences of up to **7 calendar days** you complete a self-certification form in PeopleHub when you return.

For absences longer than 7 calendar days you need a **fit note** from a doctor. Upload a copy to PeopleHub within three days of receiving it.

## 4. Company sick pay

Company sick pay is paid at your normal basic pay, depending on your length of service:

| Length of service | Company sick pay in any rolling 12 months |
|-------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Under 1 year | 2 weeks |
| 1 to 5 years | 6 weeks |
| Over 5 years | 12 weeks |

After company sick pay ends, you receive Statutory Sick Pay if you are eligible. Company sick pay is not paid for absences that were not reported as set out in section 2.

## 5. Returning to work

After every absence your line manager holds a short **return-to-work meeting**, normally on your first day back. Its purpose is to check you are well enough to work and to agree any support you need.

## 6. Frequent absence

If you have **three or more separate absences in six months**, or more than **10 working days** of absence in six months, your manager will arrange an absence review meeting with the People team.

## 7. Drivers

Drivers must tell their supervisor about any medical condition or medication that could affect their ability to drive safely, even if they feel well enough to work. Drivers may not drive while taking medication that warns against driving.
